# 06 · How the smart-glasses cancer-risk coach can credibly hit a Goal 3 indicator

Researcher: research sub-agent (Direction B, glasses) · Date: 2026-11-14
Scope: honest fit between the glasses nudge app and the AstraZeneca brief's requirement for at least one Goal 3 indicator of the Finnish national cancer strategy 2026–2035.

Terms used below
- **Goal 2** = "Vähennetään syöpätaakkaa ehkäisyn ja varhaisen toteamisen avulla" (reduce cancer burden through prevention and early detection). Strategy p. 5; see section 2.3.
- **Goal 3** = "Varmistetaan yhdenvertainen ja vaikuttava syövänhoito" (ensure equal and effective cancer treatment). Strategy p. 5; see section 2.1.
- **Sub-goal 3.1, 3.2 …** = numbered workstreams inside Goal 3 with their own measures.
- **Yhdenvertaisuus** = equality, a cross-cutting theme that sits across all four goals, not a fifth goal.
- **Hoitoonpääsy** = access to treatment; "tavoiteaika" = target time.

---

## 1. Short answer

- Our reading: nudging people toward screening or earlier symptom checks is **Goal 2 work**, because Goal 2 covers prevention and early detection (strategy p. 5; section 2.3). A judge who reads the strategy will see that.
- No option in this list gives a **measurable Goal 3 indicator** that a phone-based demo can move. The honest claim is a **proxy**: the app produces or supports data that the Goal 3 data indicator asks for, and it targets the groups the equality theme names.
- **Recommended: Option D** (equal reach and regional gap data), framed around the Goal 3 data indicator in sub-goal 3.2. Keep the glasses nudge as the intervention, and say clearly that the demo does not prove outcome change.

### 2.1 Goal 3 headline indicators (strategy p. 5)

Under the heading "Varmistetaan yhdenvertainen ja vaikuttava syövänhoito" (**[re-checked 2026-11-14]**):
- "Huonoennusteisten syöpien 5 vuoden elossaololuvut saavuttavat pohjoismaisen tason"
  - *5-year survival for poor-prognosis cancers reaches the Nordic level.*
- "Syöpäkuolleisuus pienenee 10 % (nykyisin 13 300 henkilöä vuodessa)"
  - *Cancer mortality falls 10 % (currently 13,300 people per year).*
- "Potilaat pääsevät hoitoon tavoiteaikojen puitteissa" (**[re-checked 2026-11-14]**)
  - *Patients get treatment within target times.* Footnote *** cites THL Ohjaus 21/2016 ("Hoitoonpääsyn seuranta syövän hoidossa").

### 2.2 Goal 3 measures used in this assessment

- Sub-goal 3.1 (strategy p. 33): "Potilaat pääsevät hoitoon tavoiteaikojen puitteissa." (**[re-checked 2026-11-14]**) — *Patients get treatment within target times.*
- Sub-goal 3.2 (strategy p. 36) (**[re-checked 2026-11-14]**): "Saatavilla on luotettavaa tietoa syövän diagnostiikka ja hoitokäytäntöjen alueellisista eroista, minkä avulla voidaan kaventaa eroja hoitotuloksissa."
  - *Reliable data on regional differences in diagnostics and treatment practice is available, to narrow differences in outcomes.*
  - This is the **data indicator** most relevant to Option D. It asks for data availability, not a clinical result.
- Sub-goal 3.2 also has: "Syöpäkohtaisten tietojen kirjaaminen laaturekisteriin …" (cancer-specific data entered in quality registers) and "Laaturekisterin tietopohja … (tietopohjan kattavuus 90 %)" (register coverage 90 %).

Other Goal 3 measures (3.3 psychosocial/palliative, 3.4 geriatric assessment with G8 screen, 3.5 staffing) are listed in `/home/michal/.t3/projects/vitruvian/docs/research/law/06-workforce-strategy.md` section 2. None fits a phone-glasses app well.

### 2.3 Goal 2 indicators the screening option falls under (strategy p. 5, same summary block)

Under "Vähennetään syöpätaakkaa ehkäisyn ja varhaisen toteamisen avulla" (**[re-checked 2026-11-14]**):
- "Ehkäistävien syöpien määrä laskee 1000 tapauksella vuodessa"
  - *Preventable cancers fall by 1,000 cases per year.*
- "Kaikissa syöpäseulontaohjelmissa saavutetaan 80 %:n osallistumisaste jokaisella hyvinvointialueella" (**[re-checked 2026-11-14]**)
  - *All cancer screening programmes reach 80 % participation in every wellbeing county.*
- "Vähintään 70 % syövistä diagnosoidaan levinneisyysluokissa I II"
  - *At least 70 % of cancers are diagnosed at spread classes I–II.*

### 2.4 Equality indicator (cross-cutting, not in the Goal 3 list)

Under the heading "Yhdenvertaisuus", which the strategy says runs across all goals (**[re-checked 2026-11-14]**):
- "Alueelliset ja sosioekonomiset erot syövän ilmaantuvuudessa, hoidossa ja syöpäkuolleisuudessa ovat käytettävissä palvelutoiminnassa ja niillä ohjataan toimintaa"
  - *Regional and socioeconomic differences in cancer incidence, treatment and cancer mortality are available in service operations and used to steer them.*
- Caveat: this sits under the equality theme, not under the Goal 3 headline list. A judge may accept it as Goal 3-adjacent; do not call it a Goal 3 indicator without saying so.

Source for 2.3 and 2.4: strategy PDF (URL above), p. 5 summary block.

### 2.5 Referral sub-section (Goal 2, not Goal 3)

Sub-section 2.5 "Syövät havaitaan varhaisessa" (cancers detected early) asks for "standardoidut lähetekriteerit osana kansallisia hoitoketjuja" (standardised referral criteria within national care pathways) so that "mahdollisista syöpäoireista kärsivät potilaat ohjataan nopeasti tarvittaviin diagnostisiin tutkimuksiin" (patients with possible cancer symptoms are quickly routed to diagnostic tests) (**[re-checked 2026-11-14]**). It is a Goal 2 action, so routing claims must be argued from the Goal 3 access target, not from 2.5.

## 3. Context numbers for the screening option

- 2024 national participation (Syöpärekisteri, via Potilaan Lääkärilehti): breast 81.6 %, colorectal 73.5 %, cervical 73.5 %. Regional gap between highest and lowest county: 7–14 percentage points depending on programme. Target: 80 % in every county. Source: https://www.potilaanlaakarilehti.fi/uutiset/syopaseulontoihin-osallistumisessa-isoja-alueellisia-eroja/ (secondary; figures are the registry's 2024 report).
- Registry statistics app (aggregate, public; breast and cervical 2020–2024, colorectal 2022–2024; 2025 preliminary): https://syoparekisteri.fi/tilastot/seulontatilastot/ (**UNVERIFIED**: the page returned HTTP 403 to our fetch on 2026-11-14, so the year ranges were not checked.)

### 3.1 Evidence that reminders work (for the nudge itself)

- Colorectal: a 2021 systematic review (reported as 34 randomised trials) found that letter or email reminders beat phone or text prompts. Advance notification also raised use. Source: https://doaj.org/article/664462f8c42948d4ab92367161f5fa83 (**UNVERIFIED**: the summary says "2021 systematic review of 34 RCTs"; the page was not opened to confirm the trial count).
- Breast: a randomised trial (BJC 2016) found that postal reminders a few days before the appointment increase uptake. Source: https://link.springer.com/10.1038/bjc.2015.451 (**UNVERIFIED**: sample size of 22,828 from a search summary, not checked in the paper).
- Cervical: Cochrane review CD002834 finds that invitation letters raise uptake. The 2011 version pooled 38 trials with RR 1.44 ([AAFP summary](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2012/0301/p443.html)). The 2021 update rates the evidence as moderate certainty ([Cochrane abstract](https://cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD002834.pub3/pdf/CDSR/CD002834/CD002834_abstract.pdf)). (corrected: the earlier "RR 1.71, 24 trials" was not found in any source. The 2021 trial count and pooled estimate were not checked.)
- Implication: a reminder is a proven lever; a glasses nudge is a weaker reminder than a letter and needs its own evidence. Say so.

## 5. Routing and Omaolo (evidence for Option C)

- Omaolo is an existing symptom-assessment service. After a symptom questionnaire it gives urgency and guidance on which facility to use. Source: https://journal.fi/finjehew/article/view/97020/72893 (2021 study of 15 tools; checked 2026-11-14 in the PDF text: 15 symptom checkers, and each result gives urgency, facility guidance and self-care advice).
- Finnish Chancellor of Justice matter (summarised by the ministry): a digital service cannot be the only route to public services; patients must also reach care by phone or in person; the right to a needs assessment by a health professional. Source: https://valtioneuvosto.fi/-/1271139/digitaaliset-palvelut-tukevat-julkisia-sote-palveluja-eivat-ole-niiden-portinvartijoita (**UNVERIFIED** in detail: taken from a search summary; the page was not read in full).
- Helsinki practice example (specific to Helsinki, not all counties): https://www.hel.fi/fi/sosiaali-ja-terveyspalvelut/terveydenhoito/terveysasemat/hoidon-tarpeen-arviointi-ja-ajanvaraus-terveysasemalle
- Conclusion: a glasses app that routes people to care duplicates Omaolo and needs care-provider data to prove anything. Weak to medium.

## 6. Skin-lesion example (Option B evidence, for completeness)

- Finnish guidance (secondary, press articles; Syöpäsäätiö's own page was not found in search): a suspicious mole should be checked by a doctor; the VARO letters name uneven or changing colour as a warning sign. Source: https://www.mtvuutiset.fi/artikkeli/vari-tai-kokomuutoksia-nain-tarkkailet-luomiasi/3194400 (**UNVERIFIED** as official guidance).
- Not all melanomas start in a mole; about half arise elsewhere on the skin (per the same press source; **UNVERIFIED** figure).
- UK NHS advice on a changed mole and when to see a GP: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/melanoma-skin-cancer/symptoms-of-melanoma-skin-cancer
- Use: this is a detection (Goal 2) topic and gives a "visit a doctor" nudge. It is not a Goal 3 indicator. It also raises the medical-device issue in file 07.

## 7. Recommendation

**Choose Option D. Frame the demo as an equal-reach and data tool, with the glasses nudge as the intervention.**

Why:
1. It is the only option that maps to a Goal 3 **indicator** (sub-goal 3.2 data availability), and it fits the brief's own note that "addressing the data gap is part of the challenge" ([brief, line 41](../../tracks/astrazeneca-cancer-care.md), checked 2026-11-14; the strategy is https://files.hackjunction.app/resources/Kansallinen_syopastrategia_2026_2035_FINAL.pdf).
2. The data is public and aggregate (Syöpärekisteri), which the brief allows.
3. It keeps the glasses demo honest: the nudge targets groups with low uptake, and the dashboard shows where.

Wording for the slide (suggested, to be checked by the team):
> "We help close the screening-uptake gap that the strategy's equality theme names. Public registry data shows 7–14-point county gaps against an 80 % target. We test whether reminders delivered where people already are can reach the groups that attend least. Outcome effect is not measured in this demo."

What not to claim:
- That the app improves treatment within target times or survival (Goal 3 headline indicators). It cannot show this.
- That it meets the 80 % Goal 2 target. That is a Goal 2 result, not a Goal 3 one.
- That it is clinical or diagnostic (see file 07).
