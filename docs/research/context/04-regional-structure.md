**Counties.** Finland has 21 wellbeing services counties (hyvinvointialueet). Helsinki runs its own services and is outside the 21. Source: [vm.fi, hyvinvointi- ja yhteistyöalueet](https://vm.fi/hyvinvointi-ja-yhteistyoalueet) (H).

**Centralisation decree (erikoissairaanhoidon työnjakoasetus 582/2017).** This decree decides which hospitals may do the rare, complex cancer surgeries. The aim is more cases per hospital and better results.
- The decree was made on 24.8.2017. The changes took effect on 1.7.2018. Source: [STM, sairaaloiden työnjako](https://stm.fi/artikkeli/-/asset_publisher/sairaaloiden-tyonjako-uudistuu-vuonna-2018-tiettyja-leikkauksia-ja-vaativaa-hoitoa-keskitetaan) (H).
- Volume floors (minimum cases per year per hospital): primary breast surgery about 150; colon, kidney, prostate about 70 each; rectum about 50. Source: STM link above (H for breast and "about 70"; M for rectum).
- The decree was amended on 22.9.2025, in force 1.10.2025. The amendment number could not be confirmed. Source: consultation summary (L-M) [source needed]. The law file cites "889/2025"; not confirmed here.
- A floor can be missed if the county cooperation agreement says a local central hospital is needed for on-call staffing. This waiver rule means the rule is not the same everywhere (H, STM link above).

## 2. FICAN and the regional centres

**What FICAN is.** The Finnish Cancer Center (Suomen Syöpäkeskus) is a national cancer network. It was founded in October 2019 (M) [source needed]. It was set up "guided by" the centralisation decree, to promote equal cancer care (H, [fican.fi](https://www.fican.fi/)). FICAN does not run hospitals. It coordinates. The law file notes that no statute found creates FICAN.

**Five regional centres and one national coordinating unit (H for names, [fican.fi](https://www.fican.fi/)):**

| FICAN region (Finnish name) | Host hospital (confidence) | Source |

| Läntinen (West) | TYKS, Turku (L, one source) | Search summary [source needed] |
| National coordinating unit | Location unclear. One 2019 snippet says HUS (L) [source needed] | Not on fican.fi |

- **Funding (L, old).** The state gave about €0.5M per regional centre and €0.35M to the coordinating unit at launch (about 2019). Source: search summary only [source needed].
- **2027–2031 ask (L).** FICAN asked for €139M for 2027–2031 to carry out the national strategy. This is a proposal, not approved ([Verkkouutiset](https://www.verkkouutiset.fi/a/syovan-hoidon-kulut-kasvamassa-800-miljoonalla-eurolla-naita-toimia-ehdotetaan/)).
- **Strategy link (M).** The National Cancer Strategy 2026–2035 is carried out through FICAN ([ICCP portal PDF](https://www.iccp-portal.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/National_Cancer_Strategy_2026-2035_Finland.pdf)).

**Our reading (regional gaps).** FICAN's five regions use the same names and the same host hospitals as the five cooperation areas (Eteläinen = Etelä-Suomi/HUS, Itäinen = Itä-Suomi/KYS, Sisä-Suomen = Sisä-Suomi/TAYS, Läntinen = Länsi-Suomi/TYKS, Pohjoinen = Pohjois-Suomi/OYS). So a patient's cancer-network region is set by the cooperation area their county belongs to.

## 3. Travel distance and Kela reimbursement

**Kela rules (current, [kela.fi/matkat](https://www.kela.fi/matkat), page updated 26.9.2026) (H):**

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Deductible per one-way trip | €25 (€50 for a return trip). Kela pays the rest |
| Annual cap | €300 a calendar year. After that, trips are free for the rest of the year |
| Own car | €0.33/km, minus the €25 deductible per one-way trip. Trips over 200 km need certificate SV 67 |
| Taxi | Patient pays only the deductible. Must be booked with the regional number. No per-km taxi rate given (M) |
| Claim deadline | Within 6 months of the trip |
| Payment-commitment trips | If the hospital district has a payment commitment (maksusitoumus), travel is reimbursed to where care is given (M, [Kela partner page](https://www.kela.fi/yhteistyokumppanit-terveydenhuolto-sairaanhoitokorvaukset)) |

**Key conflict (unresolved).** Kela's usual rule is the "nearest suitable care unit". Yet patients from Kainuu and Lapland go to Oulu [source needed]. The sub-agent could not confirm how these rules apply to daily radiotherapy trips. The Kela page does not mention radiotherapy (sädehoito).

**Cross-county buying (M).** Kainuu bought services from OYS worth €23.2M in the previous year (2025). This is all purchased services, not cancer care only. Source: [Kaleva, 2026](https://www.kaleva.fi/kainuun-rintasyopaleikkauksia-ei-pida-siirtaa-oulu/13682261).

**Distance and outcomes.**
- No Finnish study with travel minutes or km to radiotherapy, by region, was found (H for the gap, after searches in Finnish and English).
- Lung cancer, Vaasa Central Hospital, 2016–2020: rural vs urban mortality HR 1.47 (95% CI 1.07–2.01). Distance linked to higher mortality. Source: [Acta Oncologica article](https://www.medicaljournalssweden.se/actaoncologica/article/view/27507) (M).
- Aberdeen (Scotland): patients more than 1 hour away had worse 1-year survival ([Aberdeen news](https://www.abdn.ac.uk/news/11195/)) (M).
- Systematic review, 2024: 7 studies found no overall survival effect from distance, but less guideline-based radiotherapy with more distance (M, [AGCC news](https://www.agcc.co.uk/news-article/worse-survival-rates-and-differences-in-care-for-cancer-patients-who-live-furthest-from-hospital)).
- Tietotarjotin research project on travel reimbursement by county: page came back empty. Worth re-checking: [tietotarjotin.fi](https://tietotarjotin.fi/en/research-project/1256629/changes-in-reimbursements-for-travel-costs-paid-under-the-national-health-insurance-scheme-by-wellbeing-services-county) (L).

## 4. Primary care access and GP shortage

**Legal waiting limit (correction to the law file).**
- From 1 January 2025, the statutory limit for adult primary care (perusterveydenhuolto, avosairaanhoito) is **3 months**. Dental care is 6 months. The changes do not apply to under-23s. Source: [STM, 2025](https://stm.fi/-/perusterveydenhuollon-kiireettomaan-hoitoon-paasyn-enimmaisajat-pitenevat-vuoden-2025-alusta-lukien) (H, read by me).
- The 14-day limit from 1.9.2023 is still in place for under-23s (M, sub-agent via SOSTE document) [source needed]. Government release on the 2023 change: [valtioneuvosto.fi](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/-/1271139/perusterveydenhuollon-hoitotakuu-tiukkenee-kahteen-viikkoon-syyskuussa-2023-).

## 6. Survival, stage and screening by region

**Survival.**
- Municipal survival differences within hospital districts for 11 common cancers fell by 18%. Compared 1997–2006 with 2007–2016. Source: [MTV news, Syöpärekisteri study, 2020](https://www.mtvuutiset.fi/artikkeli/syoparekisterin-tutkimus-suomen-alueelliset-erot-syovasta-selviytymisessa-ovat-kavenneet/7854820) (M; journal not named; no education or income in the article).
- Hospital-district 5-year survival table (breast, prostate, colorectal, lung): **not found.** The sub-agent could not open the Syöpä reports.
- Breast, 2000–2022: gaps under 10 points, no region consistently worse. Source: [Tampere thesis, 2025](https://trepo.tuni.fi/handle/10024/229065) (L; master's thesis).
- Prostate: incidence spread across regions grew (SD 22.2 to 56.5 per 100,000, 1985–2004), while mortality spread shrank (SD 9.0 to 2.4, 2005–2019). Seikkula H, Kaipia A, Boström PJ, Malila N, Pitkäniemi J, Seppä K (2022), Acta Oncologica 61(10):1209–1215. [Link](https://medicaljournalssweden.se/actaoncologica/article/view/23827) (H). **Flag:** this paper also looks at municipal education levels, so do not cite it as purely regional.

**Stage at diagnosis.**
- Stage I–II vs III–IV, by region: **not found.** Do not use the data-completeness figures below as stage shares.
- Share of cases where spread (stage) was recorded, 2024: Pirkanmaa 72%, Central Finland 60%, national 42%, other areas 33–42% (M, [Verkkouutiset](https://www.verkkouutiset.fi/a/syopatiedoissa-isot-alueelliset-erot-yksi-seutu-erottuu/)). This is about recording quality. It is not a stage mix.

## 8. Biggest unknowns and what would settle them

1. **Whether regional survival or stage differs by hospital district.** No public table was found, and the Syöpä reports were not opened. This is the single biggest unknown. Without it, the gap in outcome cannot be shown by region, only the gap in access.
   - Would settle it: Finnish Cancer Registry hospital-district survival tables, and stage mix by region.
2. **Travel effect on radiotherapy.** No Finnish travel-time data by region was found.
   - Would settle it: Kela data on travel costs by county (tietotarjotin project), or THL linked data on distance to units.
3. **County-level primary-care waits and GP vacancies.** Only national numbers and two county points were found.
   - Would settle it: THL Sampo monthly access data by county; KT vacancy data by county.
4. **Public vs occupational vs private use by region.** The 2025 survey data was not found.
   - Would settle it: Terveys-barometri or FinSote by county and income.
5. **County closed-account results 2023–2025.** Only national totals and a few counties were found; sources disagree.
   - Would settle it: VM and Valtiokonttori closed-account tables by county.
6. **Which radiotherapy units exist at KYS, TYKS, Jyväskylä, Seinäjoki and Pori.**
   - Would settle it: THL or each hospital's site.
