### Goal 3 (checked)

Goal 3 is **"Varmistetaan yhdenvertainen ja vaikuttava syövänhoito"** (ensure equal and effective cancer care). It has three parts:
- 3.1 national care guidelines and care pathways that give equal, fast (viiveetön) and effective care
- 3.2 a **national cancer care quality registry**, plus systematic review of results and peer review
- 3.3 rehab, psychosocial support, health social work and palliative care

Equal access is also a theme across all goals. The strategy says diagnosis, treatment and rehab should be available equally and without delay across the country.

Sources:
- Strategy summary (Finnish): https://valtioneuvosto.fi/-/1271139/yhteinen-suunta-ihmislahtoiseen-ja-vaikuttavaan-syovan-ehkaisyyn-ja-hoitoon-kansallinen-syopastrategia-on-julkaistu (published 10.11.2025)
- Strategy PDF "Yhdessä syöpää vastaan": https://stm.fi/documents/1271139/1329769/Kansallinen_syopastrategia_2026_2035.pdf (seen in search, not read in full)
- STM strategy page: https://stm.fi/en/national-cancer-strategy

Watch out: one search summary also lists targets such as **70% of cancers found at stage I–II by 2035** and **90% of patients with access to a CC-level (OECI) cancer centre**. The summary did not say whether these sit under Goal 3. Check the PDF before using them.

## 3. AstraZeneca in Finland and the Nordics

### 3a. Public priorities (global, mostly)

- **Lung health equity (2025):** AZ says it will find barriers along the patient journey, then address disparities "at the postal code level" (page wording, checked 2026-11-14). Source: https://www.astrazeneca.com/media-centre/articles/2025/our-commitment-to-improving-lung-health-equity.html
- **Lung Ambition Alliance:** AZ-led flagship on screening and early diagnosis, "active in 50 countries". Same source.
- **AI X-ray lung screening** in low- and middle-income countries with Qure.ai; goal of 5 million screened by end of 2025 (result not verified). Same source.
- **Health equity:** screening and early detection sit under "healthcare delivery"; cancer, lung and renal are named priority areas; "more than 100 partnerships". Source: https://www.astrazeneca.com/sustainability/access-to-healthcare.html (as at Dec 2025).

### 3b. Finland specifically

- **Only Finnish pilot found:** a **kidney (CKD) screening pilot** (not cancer) with Pirkanmaa wellbeing county, Fimlab lab and the University of Eastern Finland health economics group. Late 2024 to summer 2025; 8,248 invited, 4,018 took part, 236 flagged. Source (Finnish): https://www.mynewsdesk.com/fi/astrazeneca/pressreleases/ensimmaeinen-kroonisen-munuaistaudin-seulontapilotti-onnistui-ja-toi-hyvinvointialueille-tyoekalun-seulonnan-vaikuttavuuden-arviointiin-3408579 (Oct 2025).
- **No Finnish cancer or lung-screening partnership** with AZ found.
- **Lung cancer in Finland:** a University of Helsinki study (7 Nov 2025) found Finnish lung cancer outcomes worse than other Nordic countries (later diagnosis, less early CT use, no hospital outcome tracking). It was done with **MSD Finland**, not AZ. Source: https://www.helsinki.fi/fi/uutiset/terveys/miksi-suomessa-keuhkosyopaan-kuollaan-aiemmin-kuin-pohjoismaissa
- **National lung programme 2026–2035** (Filha ry, from May 2026): aims to "improve and equalize diagnosis and treatment" of lung cancer. Partner list not checked (PDF not readable). Source: https://www.filha.fi/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Valtakunnallinen-keuhkosyopaohjelma-2026-2035_180526_preprint.pdf
- **AZ Nordics leadership:** the only name found is Emelie Antoni, named Country President Nordics & Sweden in June 2023. Probably out of date. Source: https://www.mynewsdesk.com/fi/astrazeneca/pressreleases/astrazeneca-welcomes-new-country-president-for-the-nordics-3260000

### 3c. What AZ probably wants from this track (inference, not stated by AZ)

1. **A regional pilot the county can keep and measure.** Based on the CKD pilot: a county, a lab and a university tested screening value and built an evaluation tool.
2. **Targeting underserved people, not the average.** Based on the postcode language in 3a. This fits the deck's point that inequity is structural.
3. **Earlier diagnosis and pathway fixes, not more drugs.** Screening and early detection are AZ's stated health-delivery focus. The national strategy also puts early detection in its goals. The deck's biggest measured gap is before the specialist (deck p. 11).
4. **A fit with the national lung programme.** Unverified, since the PDF was unreadable.
5. **Careful framing.** AZ has low visible presence in Finnish cancer policy. Inference: it may prefer a neutral, data-led framing over brand-led work. Also: the deck speaker declares AZ consulting (deck p. 2), so the link is public and should be stated openly.
