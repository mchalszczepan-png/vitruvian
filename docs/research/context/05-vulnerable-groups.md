# Vulnerable groups and cancer care in Finland

Date: 2026-11-14. Research only. Scope: the groups named in `docs/tracks/astrazeneca-insights-deck.md` (pp. 5, 7, 9), plus digital access and client fees.

**How to read this**
- **Strong:** national register or large peer-reviewed study (abstract read at least).
- **Medium:** one study, survey, official report, or registry press release.
- **Weak:** news, NGO page, derived estimate, search summary only, or non-Finnish.
- **Not found:** our searches did not find it. It may still exist.
- Terms: PMC = free full-text article ID. PMID = PubMed ID. SIR = observed vs expected cases (1 = same as average). HR = hazard ratio (above 1 = higher risk).

## Quick view

| Group | Biggest gap we found | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People with severe mental illness | Cancer death risk HR 1.42 (men), 1.47 (women) vs no mental illness. Stage did not explain it. ([source](#8-people-with-severe-mental-illness)) | Strong |
| Migrants, especially Africa-born and Somali-origin | Cervical screening: Africa-born 46% vs Finnish-born 73%. Somali-origin 19% vs Finns 67% (small study). ([source](#4-migrants-and-people-who-speak-neither-finnish-nor-swedish)) | Medium |
| Lower education | Surgery 36% vs 51%; more stage IV. Deck gives cancer deaths 47% vs 27% (crude shares, not adjusted; see [Zai et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12551501/)). | Strong (Zai et al.) |

**Headline gap:** the deck's lymphoma gap (DLBCL lymphoma: 207 vs 123 days to treatment, public vs private care, [Kuitunen et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/cam4.71920)) is confirmed for the route split. The paper reports median 127 days via primary healthcare vs 76 via private or occupational care (p=0.027, n=160); Figure 1 shows 207 vs 123 days to treatment start ([Kuitunen et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/cam4.71920)).

## Gaps and checks

1. **Deck DLBCL numbers (207 vs 123 days):** the route split is confirmed by [Kuitunen 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/cam4.71920) (median 127 vs 76 days, p=0.027; Figure 1 shows 207 vs 123). Tokola 2025 not found.
2. **Zai et al.:** numbers come from search summaries. BMJ and PubMed pages would not load. Reconcile deck's 47% vs 27% with HR 1.13 / 1.14.
3. **Named sources not checked:** "MigLeTe", FinMonik, and FinSote / HYTE survey results were not found by the sweeps. Ask for exact names.
4. **Unread documents:** Finlex text of the Client Fees Act (734/1992) and § numbers; Statistics Finland digital-skill tables by education and income; Finnish Cancer Registry annual reports (stage and screening by education); THL Roma report 15/2018; Health 2000 data; ESMO Open full text.
5. **Weak spots:** Sámi and Roma data are old or thin. No Finnish cancer data for homeless people, LGBTQ+ people, or people with intellectual disability.

**Biggest gaps (for the deck's question):** severe mental illness, migrants from Africa / Somali origin, and lower education.
