# 13 — Pirkanmaa stage-recording change: what changed, and what it did

Date: 2026-11-14. Scope: research only, no code.
Tags: **READ** = full text read (by me, or in an earlier pass noted in 07/09). **SNIPPET** = search summary or highlight only. **UNVERIFIED** = claim I could not check.
Terms: Pirha = Pirkanmaan hyvinvointialue (Pirkanmaa wellbeing county). TAYS = Tampere University Hospital. CGI = IT vendor. MDT = multidisciplinary team meeting. FICAN = national cancer centre. Registry = Suomen Syöpärekisteri.

---

## Verdict (3 lines)

1. **Data got much better.** Pirkanmaa's clinical notifications rose from 42% (2022) to 80% (2024) [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2025/06/26/yhteistyo-toi-parannusta-syoparekisteri-ilmoituksiin/]. Its spread-data coverage was 72% in 2024 (71.9%), against 41.9% nationally [READ, https://syoparekisteri.fi/assets/themes/ssy3/factsheets/syopa_2024_tilastoraportti.html].
2. **No patient outcome is documented.** I found no survival, time-to-treatment, MDT or stage-shift result for Pirkanmaa. "Data better" is shown. "Patients did better" is not.
3. **Few copies so far.** A few other areas were involved. National plans exist (Kanta by 2029 [Finlex, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2008/668]; 90% automated by 2030 [STM, https://stm.fi/documents/1271139/1329769/Kansallinen_syopastrategia_2026_2035_FINAL.pdf/05ad3c9d-d8ba-a629-3e90-e8d10f4f5bbc?t=1767863923329]; a quality register), but the 2027 money was not in the budget proposal (STT, 24.8.2026, https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/72255408/puutteita-syovanhoidossa-ei-voida-korjata-ilman-rahoitusta?lang=fi).

## Do not mix these numbers

- **80%** (2024) = share of cancers with a clinical notification. **72%** (2024) = share of cancers with spread data from any source. Different measures. [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2025/06/26/yhteistyo-toi-parannusta-syoparekisteri-ilmoituksiin/; SNIPPET, https://www.syopajarjestot.fi/uutinen/syovan-levinneisyystiedoissa-suuria-alueellisia-eroja-suomessa/]
- **42%** (2022) = Pirkanmaa's clinical notification rate. **41.9%** (2024) = national spread-data coverage [READ, https://syoparekisteri.fi/assets/themes/ssy3/factsheets/syopa_2024_tilastoraportti.html]. Same number, different things.
- The three regional parts in the 2024 factsheet add to 40.8%, not 41.9%. Unexplained. [READ, https://syoparekisteri.fi/assets/themes/ssy3/factsheets/syopa_2024_tilastoraportti.html]

## 1. What Pirkanmaa changed

**What.** A new IT workflow. The clinical cancer notification is made inside the diagnosis entry, so the clinician files it as part of normal care. Spread at diagnosis is captured at that point. [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2025/06/26/yhteistyo-toi-parannusta-syoparekisteri-ilmoituksiin/]

**Who.**
- Built by CGI together with the registry, Pirha and "a few other wellbeing areas". [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2025/06/26/yhteistyo-toi-parannusta-syoparekisteri-ilmoituksiin/]
- The software was developed over "several years" in regular meetings. Kuopio University Hospital and Varsinais-Suomi wellbeing area were also in the working group. [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2025/06/26/yhteistyo-toi-parannusta-syoparekisteri-ilmoituksiin/]

**When.**
- Baseline 2022 (42%), result 2024 (80%). Registry article dated 26 Jun 2025. [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2025/06/26/yhteistyo-toi-parannusta-syoparekisteri-ilmoituksiin/]
- The same 2024 figure appears in the registry's Tietotilinpäätös 2025, which credits cooperation with the registry, the IT vendor and a few other areas. [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/tietoa-syoparekisterista/tietotilinpaatos-2025/]

**Same period, national changes (overlap).**
- THL binding decision THL/5273/5.03.00/2023 says wellbeing areas must send the data. [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2024/04/16/thl-on-tehnyt-paatoksen-syoparekisteriin-toimitettavista-tiedoista/; decision text not read, blocked per 09]
- Data transfers ("tietotoimitukset") started in 2024. Syöpäjärjestöt says notification activity rose markedly after they began. The registry talked with areas that had no transfers. [source needed]
- The registry credits the Pirkanmaa workflow. **How much of the rise is the workflow and how much the national decision is not shown.** [UNVERIFIED]

**TAYS breast cancer.** 97% of breast cancer patients have a clinical notification. The registry says the process was built into multidisciplinary (MDT) meetings. [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2025/06/26/yhteistyo-toi-parannusta-syoparekisteri-ilmoituksiin/]

**Cost.** Not found for the Pirkanmaa tool. [UNVERIFIED] National only: +€400,000 this budget year for quality-register work. [READ, https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/72255408/puutteita-syovanhoidossa-ei-voida-korjata-ilman-rahoitusta?lang=fi]

**People (public roles, as printed).**
- Janne Pitkäniemi: director, Suomen Syöpärekisteri [source needed]. Set the 70% target for spread data [READ, https://syoparekisteri.fi/assets/themes/ssy3/factsheets/syopa_2024_tilastoraportti.html].
- Tomi Mäkelä: director, FICAN. Quoted on the €139M roadmap. [source needed]
- Sirpa Rainesalo: named in a Tivi article as Pirha's chief medical director, on a new CGI patient system. Article date not confirmed; check it is current. [source needed]
- Nina Airisto: author of the registry article. Role not shown. [SNIPPET, https://syoparekisteri.fi/2025/06/26/yhteistyo-toi-parannusta-syoparekisteri-ilmoituksiin/]

**Caution (inference, UNVERIFIED).** Better recording can shift the stage distribution with no change in patients. A 2020 simulation found that registry completeness can bias survival comparisons. [SNIPPET, Br J Cancer, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41416-020-01196-7] A 2022→2024 "stage gain" in Pirkanmaa may be partly recording. Test this before using it.

## 3. Did other counties copy it, or plan to?

- **Other areas.** "A few" areas took part in the design. The best cooperation area is Sisä-Suomi at 60%. The Syöpäjärjestöt summary calls Sisä-Suomi the "most complete" but gives Pirkanmaa a higher 72%. This conflict is not resolved. [SNIPPET, https://www.syopajarjestot.fi/uutinen/syovan-levinneisyystiedoissa-suuria-alueellisia-eroja-suomessa/]
- **Kanta / automatic feed.** THL act §5 j: THL must collect data through national IT services by **31.12.2029**. [READ, https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2008/668] Whether a cancer-specific Kanta feed exists: **not verified**. [UNVERIFIED]
- **Strategy 2026–2035.** Target: 90% of clinical notifications automated by 2030. [READ, https://stm.fi/documents/1271139/1329769/Kansallinen_syopastrategia_2026_2035_FINAL.pdf/05ad3c9d-d8ba-a629-3e90-e8d10f4f5bbc?t=1767863923329] Goal codes 3.2b and 3.2d: **not confirmed**. [UNVERIFIED]
- **Quality register.** THL + registry + FICAN project since 2024 [source needed]. Pilot: lung cancer (report Dec 2024; second report 2026) [SNIPPET, https://www.verkkouutiset.fi/a/keuhkosyopa-todetaan-usein-liian-myohaan-vain-joka-neljas-saa-parantavaa-hoitoa/]. A myeloma pilot (2025) is not confirmed [source needed] (corrected: earlier text listed myeloma as a second pilot). Colorectal, prostate and breast added later [source needed].
- **Money.** FICAN roadmap: €139M for 2027–2031 (corrected from 2026–2031) [READ, https://valtioneuvosto.fi/-/1271139/yhteinen-suunta-ihmislahtoiseen-ja-vaikuttavaan-syovan-ehkaisyyn-ja-hoitoon-kansallinen-syopastrategia-on-julkaistu]. The budget proposal (STT, 24.8.2026, https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/72255408/puutteita-syovanhoidossa-ei-voida-korjata-ilman-rahoitusta?lang=fi) has +€400,000 for this year and nothing for next year (2027).
- **Data model.** FICAN says a future register needs structured, harmonised data (OMOP, openEHR or FHIR) produced routinely by wellbeing areas, plus enabling law. [source needed]
- **Pirha platform.** Pirha is moving to one CGI patient system, with specialist care planned for 2026. If the notification tool runs on it, other areas on the same system could copy it. **Link not verified.** [SNIPPET, https://www.sttinfo.fi/tiedote/70749617/pirkanmaan-hyvinvointialue-siirtyy-kayttamaan-yhteista-potilastietojarjestelmaa-ensimmaisena-jarjestelma-vaihtuu-pirkkalassa-ja-vesilahdessa-tammikuussa-2025?lang=fi; SNIPPET, https://www.cgi.com/fi/fi/uutinen/cgin-omni360-potilastietojarjestelma-nyt-kaytossa-varha-pirha-pohjois-savon-hyvinvointialue (CGI, 16.12.2025); UNVERIFIED] Specialist-care date [source needed].

## 4. Comparable evidence elsewhere

Short answer: registry feedback helped where it sat inside a full package (guidelines, audit, pathways). Recording alone is not shown to improve outcomes.

- **Denmark, lung cancer.** National registry since 2000, with quality indicators, quarterly reports and yearly audit. 1-year survival 36.6% (2003) → 42.7% (2011). 5-year survival 9.8% → 12.1%. 5-year survival after surgery 39.5% → 48.1%. Authors credit guidelines, data quality, reports, audit and commitment together. No control group. [SNIPPET, https://searchworks.jioinstitute.edu.in/articles/cmedm__24457234; Jakobsen et al.; PMID 24457234 read from the record URL, not the snippet]
- **Denmark and Sweden, TNM.** TNM mandatory since 2004. Lung cancer 2014–16: TNM available for 96% in Denmark and Sweden, 78% in Norway. Finland not in that dataset. [READ, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11332511/; Engholm 2024; SNIPPET, https://medicaljournalssweden.se/actaoncologica/article/view/35232 for the 2004 TNM date]
- **Denmark, pathways.** A national cancer patient pathway (2007 policy) did not show a clear overall stage shift in a GP-based cohort around its 2008–2009 rollout: OR 0.88 (95% CI 0.73 to 1.06) for all cancers. Patients referred urgently through the pathway had lower odds of local cancer: OR 0.77 (95% CI 0.62 to 0.94). (corrected: earlier text said stage gains came with a 2004 plan.) [SNIPPET, Jensen et al., BJGP 2016, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4871309; 2007 policy, SNIPPET, https://content.ugeskriftet.dk/sites/default/files/scientific_article_files/2018-08/a5322.pdf]
- **Norway, pathways (2015).** Stage distribution improved gradually from 2007 to 2016. No sharp change at launch. [WebSearch summary, not read; the only Norway hit is a waiting-time paper, https://aminer.org/pub/5cf4efd23a55acbf91f44c2d/decreasing-waiting-time-for-treatment-before-and-during-implementation-of-cancer-patient] [source needed] So a care change does not quickly shift stage.
- **England.** Stage completeness above 80% for major cancers (2012 diagnoses), which allowed survival-by-stage analysis. [SNIPPET, BJC, https://www.nature.com/articles/bjc201549; NCRAS profile: no URL, source needed] Better data made analysis possible. The source does not show an outcome gain from recording alone.
- **UK National Lung Cancer Audit.** 1-year survival 41% (2019) → 50% (2023). The 2019 baseline differs across sources (41%, 45%, 46%). Early-stage share rose from 30% (2021) to 37% (2023) (corrected: earlier text gave no stage figures and cited PMC12622281, which was not found). Cause not proven. [SNIPPET, https://www.natcan.org.uk/news/blog-state-of-the-nation-2025-progress-pressures-and-priorities-in-lung-cancer-care-2/]
