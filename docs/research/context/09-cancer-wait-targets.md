Notes on the STM 2010 wording:
- The 6 weeks are a maximum ("maksimiaika"). They are not a law.
- The report says cancer care has had no set deadlines before, and that none were set by law. Quote: "Näin sitä koskevia määräaikoja ei aiemmin ole määritelty eikä niistä ole säädetty." [READ]
- The 6-week clock includes the time for diagnostic tests. [READ]

Legal base today:
- Health care guarantee (hoitotakuu) rules: specialist care within 6 months of the assessed need. [SNIPPET] (https://minilex.fi/a/hoitotakuun-laiminlyönti)
- Primary care: 3 months for adults 23 and over, and 14 days for under 23s, from 1.1.2025. [READ] (https://stm.fi/-/miten-hoitotakuuta-koskevia-saannoksia-sovelletaan-stm-julkaisi-ohjeet-hyvinvointialueille, read in an earlier pass)
- No law sets cancer-specific deadlines. [READ, STM 2010:6 wording above]

Measurement points that matter when comparing:
- Start point: referral, suspicion, or the first visit. The 2010 target uses the referral.
- Measure: median or average. Calendar or working days.
