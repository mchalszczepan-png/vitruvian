# 05 - AI referral triage and Google health tool landscape

Research only. Date of research: 2026-11-14. Tags: READ = page opened (fetch summary), SNIPPET = search result text only, UNVERIFIED = memory or no source found, [source needed] = no source found after search.

## Summary

1. Most AI referral triage that exists runs in the hospital, after the referral is sent. The GP-side fast lane is almost empty. The best independent trial (LungIMPACT, [SNIPPET](https://www.auntminnie.com/clinical-news/digital-x-ray/article/15820374/study-finds-that-ai-fails-to-speed-lung-cancer-diagnosis)) found AI worklist prioritisation did not speed up diagnosis.
2. Google's "Gemini Decisions API" ([launched 2026-11-11](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/11/google-gemini-decisions-api-gemini-decision-1/)) is a general closed-set scoring API. It has no stated health, medical or regulated use ([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/), READ). Our design would make it a clinical decision support tool.
3. LLM triage studies show wording and framing sensitivity, overconfidence and output variance. They do not show the GP-side fast-lane design works. No study of LLM-drafted referral letters for accuracy was found.

## Notes on Google tools

- **Gemini Decisions API.** READ ([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/), fetch summary). Input: text, images, audio, video, PDF. Output: a probability per yes/no question; winner plus probabilities for multiple choice; weighted rubric score. Price: $0.10 per 1M input tokens, $0 output (per Google, via fetch summary). Latency 150-250 ms. A free no-cost tier exists in Google AI Studio [source needed]. Region and preview or GA status are not stated. Confidence scores are meant for sending ambiguous cases to a human. The article does not mention health, medical or clinical use.
- **Status.** Launched 2026-11-11 (SNIPPET: [marktechpost.com](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/11/google-gemini-decisions-api-gemini-decision-1/), [cryptobriefing.com](https://cryptobriefing.com/google-gemini-decisions-api-launch/)). Secondary coverage varies; check AI Studio docs before relying on it.
- **Regulatory point.** A clinical rule that flags risk for a referral decision is likely decision-support software. Under MDR Annex VIII Rule 11, most such software is Class IIa or higher [source needed for a URL]. Under the EU AI Act, AI in Class IIa+ devices is high-risk (Article 6(1)). (corrected) The Digital Omnibus on AI moves the Annex I date to 2 Aug 2028 (was 2027-08-02 in the earlier draft of this note). Search summaries say the Omnibus is in force; final publication was not confirmed ([SNIPPET: meddeviceguide.com](https://meddeviceguide.com/blog/eu-ai-act-omnibus-amendment-2026-medical-device-impact-guide), [SNIPPET: taylorwessing.com](https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/international-life-sciences-newsletter/life-sciences-legal-lens-vol-2/update-ai-enabled-medical-devices-and-ivds-confirmed-as-high-risk)).
- **Fimea.** SNIPPET ([regask.com](https://regask.com/finlands-fimea-issues-guidance-on-eu-ai-act-requirements-for-ai-enabled-medical-devices-effective-january-19-2026/)): Fimea published AI guidance on 2026-01-19. Fimea is the market surveillance authority for AI medical devices ([fimea.fi](https://fimea.fi/en/medical-devices), role for AI devices [source needed]). Could not open the guidance. Check fimea.fi directly. Valvira role not found.
- **Gemma (local).** Our local option. Open weights, runs on local hardware (UNVERIFIED for hardware limits and licence terms; check Gemma terms before shipping).

## Implications for our Gemini Decisions + local Gemma design

- Risk: sending Finnish patient data to a cloud Decisions API needs a data-processing agreement, data residency, and a Finnish health-data legal check (UNVERIFIED: Finnish health-data law). Keep the summary and draft local with Gemma; test the API on de-identified text first.
- Risk: the rule-plus-score design turns into a medical device. Plan for a MDR and AI Act file, not only a hackathon demo.
- Opportunity: Decisions API gives calibrated probabilities per risk factor ([Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)). That maps well to our "one question per risk factor" design and to a fallback-to-human threshold. Our reading: calibration is Google's own claim until we test it.
- Risk: framing and wording change LLM output ([SNIPPET, Omar et al.](https://research.bidmc.org/radiology-research/publications/ai-assisted-rheumatology-triage-changes-referral-framing)). Fix the question wording and test it against Finnish GP notes.
- Opportunity: no GP-side Finnish tool found. The hospital-side ÄLLI shows the need; a GP-side fast lane would sit upstream of it.

## Gap left

- No tool found that acts at the GP desk for suspected cancer and measures the total wait from suspicion to treatment (P1 and P3 together).
- No Finnish-language evaluation of any LLM triage or referral tool.
- No study of LLM-drafted referral letters for accuracy or bias.
- Dragon Copilot, Med-PaLM/AMIE and Vertex healthcare are not checked.

## White space

- GP-side urgency rule with fast-lane decision (none found in Finland or UK).
- Measurement of total wait, suspicion to treatment, with AI prioritisation (LungIMPACT shows queue-only fixes may fail).
- Finnish-language referral letter quality and bias tests.
- Apotti/Lifecare/Omni360 integration for referral drafts (none found).
- Kanta-linked referral data access (none found).
