**One line:** tools exist for each piece somewhere, but nobody joins them up for Finland's public route: no total cancer clock, no GP fast lane with a scan, no automatic stage feed, and no split by entry route.

## White space (what nobody does)

1. **A total clock** from suspicion (or first GP visit) to first treatment, published by Finnish county. Nordic countries publish part-clocks that start at referral or diagnosis ([01 S13–S16](01-pathway-wait-tracking.md#sources)).
2. **Split by entry route** (public GP vs occupational/private) or income, in any country's cancer wait report.
3. **GP-side fast lane that ends in an action**: red-flag rule + EHR prompt + scan the GP can book. UK and Australia have prompts without routes; Scotland has a route without a prompt ([02](02-gp-risk-decision-support.md)). Finland has neither in public care.
4. **Cancer red flags in Finland's existing channel** (EBMeDS in Apotti) – none found.
5. **Finnish-language tests** of any cancer prompt, LLM triage or referral draft.
6. **Equity data** for any risk tool or symptom checker; the people we care about are excluded from the evidence.
7. **Pre-diagnosis wait capture**: apps cover patients already in treatment.
8. **Automatic clinical stage notification** from a Finnish EHR, with sign-off and owner; no Finnish-language stage extractor exists.

## Implications for package A (GP fast lane + Gemini Decisions prompt, county wait view, auto stage report)

1. **Lead with the rule, not the AI.** Every prompt-only tool (QCancer, FHT) showed little effect ([02](02-gp-risk-decision-support.md)); the novelty is prompt + bookable scan in public care, which nobody does. Frame Gemini Decisions as the delivery of a county rule.
2. **The wait view is the strongest white space:** a total, route-split clock with a crowd-out check. LungIMPACT shows prioritising a queue alone does not cut waits ([05](05-ai-referral-triage.md)), so measure the whole path.
3. **Stage report = a prefilled notification draft a named clinician signs**, built from pathology/imaging (mCODE-style fields), not an NLP accuracy showcase. Sweden and Pirkanmaa show the send step and ownership matter most; Kanta reporting due by 2029 ([Finlex 668/2008, §5 j](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2008/668)) gives a planned path. Show completeness by county (the registry publishes it: [2024 factsheet](https://syoparekisteri.fi/assets/themes/ssy3/factsheets/syopa_2024_tilastoraportti.html)) as the before/after.
4. **Risks to state up front:** a patient flag is a likely MDR class IIa device (AI Act high-risk) [05 regulatory point, source needed]; cloud API needs data-residency checks, so keep patient text local (Gemma) and send only de-identified questions; Finnish wording must be fixed and tested.
5. **Don't overlap HUS ÄLLI** (hospital-side referral sorting): position ours upstream at the GP and ask HUS about it.
