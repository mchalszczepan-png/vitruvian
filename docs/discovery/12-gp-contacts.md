## Message templates

Placeholders in [brackets]. Send only to a general or role contact, or to a GP who has published a professional contact. Do not call a surgery's patient line.

### Cold email or LinkedIn message (EN)

> **Subject:** 10 minutes on suspected-cancer referrals (GP)
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm [your name] from a team at the Junction 2026 hackathon in Espoo. We are learning how GPs in public health centres send suspected lung, bowel and prostate cancer referrals. We want to hear about your day, not about patients.
>
> Could you spare 10 minutes by phone this week, or answer 3 short questions by email?
>
> We won't ask for any patient data. Thanks, [your name], [team]
