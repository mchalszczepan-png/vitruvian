# Secondary customer evidence: cancer care delays in Finland

> **Secondary sources only. No interviews, no testimony. Not customer-backed.**
> Written 2026-11-14 for [02-problem-map.md](02-problem-map.md). Every row has a source link, a source type and a pathway step.

## How to read this

- **Secondary** = published by someone else (research, patient-group survey, news or TV report). Not our own interviews.
- **Pathway steps** = the 8-step delay model in [deck p.10](../tracks/astrazeneca-insights-deck.md): 1 symptoms begin · 2 deciding to seek care · 3 first contact · 4 physician assessment · 5 tests and referral · 6 diagnosis · 7 team plan · 8 treatment start. **0** = prevention, before step 1.
- **P1–P11 / U1–U11** = problem and unknown labels from [02-problem-map.md](02-problem-map.md).
- **Quotes** are copied from the source text. Where I give an English gloss, it is my translation and is not a quote.
- **Method:** several web searches (Finnish and English). Most sources were opened and checked on the page. Search summaries were used only to find sources, never as evidence. Two rows are news reports of studies or surveys we did not open (S9, S2 survey details); each row says so.

## Patterns

1. **Money and access come first.** 23% avoided care, and four in five of them cite money (S2). Top barriers are money, a suitable doctor and distance (S1). Income is linked to which route people used first in the lymphoma cohort (S5). The deck notes say income was not significant for the delay itself ([deck notes, p.11 corrections](../tracks/astrazeneca-insights-deck.md)). Several sources agree on cost and access.
2. **Delay is not only patient fear.** Over half say symptoms were not taken seriously, and a third needed three or more contacts (S1). GP-side sources point the same way: awareness gaps, staff shortages and missed symptoms (S10, S3, S11). Patient delay and system delay overlap, and no Finnish source splits them.
3. **Lower education means less treatment and more death from cancer** (S6, S4). The later-stage point is in the deck notes (p.15), not in the S6 quotes above. This is the strongest source, but it is a register study. It shows the gap, not the cause, and no Finnish study with patient voices explains it.
4. **Prevention may reach the same people, not the ones who need it most.** Those who start a Finnish digital lifestyle app are more educated, healthier and already use e-services (S7). A glasses coach that needs setup or an app may face the same skill gate. Reach to lower-SES adults is not tested.

Count: 13 external sources. Patient-side Finnish sources: S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, S9, S13 (not all are independent). Repeated coverage of one survey counts once.

## What this changes in the problem map (02-problem-map.md)

- **P1 (late first contact):** Now backed by 3 Finnish sources (S1, S2, S3) on cost and not being taken seriously. Confidence can move from "medium that a delay exists" to "medium that cost and recognition are part of it". Still low on which step is most fixable. Label all of it secondary.
- **P2 (wait for a physician):** S2 splits the view (44% hard, 49% easy). S10 gives the staffing side. Do not write "most people cannot get a GP". Confidence medium.
- **P3 (referral step):** The GP evidence (S11) is UK. No Finnish source. Keep confidence low. The new Finnish GP gap is still open.
- **P8 (education gradient):** The map said the "27% vs 47%" metric was not defined. S6 defines it: share who died of cancer during 2000–2019. Use that wording. Surgery 51% vs 36% is from the deck notes (crude shares). The S6 page returned 403 in the 2026-11-14 re-check, so it is not re-confirmed here. The paper's adjusted cancer-death hazard ratio (1.13 men, 1.14 women, low vs high education) is in the deck notes (p.15) and is the safer headline.
- **DLBCL day numbers (deck p.11):** S5 confirms the direction (private or work route is faster; route is linked to age and income). The day numbers are in the paper: 207 and 123 are Figure 1 labels (cumulative time to treatment start; the figure does not say which bar is which, read from the PDF 2026-11-14). The paper's text gives the route test as median 127 vs 76 days (n=48 vs 39, p=0.027), a different cut from the figure's 207 vs 123. Keep the map's warning. Read the full text before any slide uses those numbers.
- **P11 (prevention does not reach lower-SES adults):** Moves from "qualitative only" to one Finnish uptake study (S7). That is uptake only, no outcome. New candidate sub-problem for a human to decide: **digital skill gate** (e-service use was the strongest predictor).
- **New candidate, not in the map:** language and migrant barriers. Only S12 (Denmark, qualitative, family interpreters). No Finnish source. Could be a low-confidence row or an interview question. Human decides.
- **P7 (no public county cancer data):** [S3](https://yle.fi/a/74-20251023) says Finland has no national cancer quality registry (not re-checked in the 2026-11-14 session; the same point appears in a search summary of an STT release, see [law07 §3](../research/law/07-verification.md)). This supports P7 from a second source.
- **Interview guide:** These findings are hypotheses for guide §2 and §4 prompts, not testimony. They do not confirm or kill Group 1–4 hypotheses.

## Gaps and limits

- **No Finnish qualitative study of patient delay found.** Several searches (Finnish and English) returned only quantitative work.
- **No Potilasliitto survey on this topic found.** No Finnish study on migrants and cancer delay found.
- **No Finnish GP survey on suspected-cancer referral found.** Finnish referral rules (Käypä hoito, legal time limits) were not checked here. See [law research](../research/law/00-synthesis.md).
- **Two different patient surveys:** S1 is Suomen Syöpäpotilaat ry (684 answers). S2 is Syöpäjärjestöt. Do not merge them. Sample method for both is not in the articles read.
- **Date conflict:** S3's URL suggests 23 Oct 2025. The page metadata says 12 Oct 2026. Use "2025 or 2026" until checked.
- **Source disagreement:** S7 gives 18.5% uptake in the abstract and 18.2% in the discussion. Both kept.
- **S5 details not checked:** sample of 160 and seven hospitals, and funding by Incyte, come from a search summary. Not checked in the full text.
- **Not used (snippet only, not verified):** Norwegian immigrant waiting-time registry [source needed]; OECD 2024 migrant stage review (via a 2025 article) [source needed]; Lääkäriliitto 2021 survey [source needed]; THL access survey [source needed].
- **Deck claims cited, not checked:** deck p.8 (lifestyle risk clusters in lower SES), deck p.10 model ([deck notes](../tracks/astrazeneca-insights-deck.md)).
