## Do not miss

1. **Final submission deadline: Sunday 15 Nov, 10:00 sharp.** The form closes automatically; no late submissions. The portal clock showed about 28 h left at 13:30 today.
2. **Press "Submit Final".** Draft projects are **not reviewed**. Our project "Vitruvian" is still **Draft** on the dashboard.
3. **Pitch video: maximum 2 minutes, strictly enforced** (guide: "not a second longer"). Public link (YouTube/Vimeo). The "3 minutes" we assumed applies only to the live top-5 pitch (below).
4. **Be at the venue for live peer review on Sunday.** No participation = **not eligible for the main prize**.
5. **No code written before the event.** Disqualification if pre-written code for this idea is used, or if the project is tied to an existing company. Open-source libraries are fine. Our docs/research are not code, but keep commits in the event window and the repo history clean.
6. **Source link is required** and every submission is licensed **MIT**; organizers must be able to access the code.
7. **Gemini credits form: one person per team, only up to 400 teams.** Do it now (see `01-sponsor-resources.md`).

## Judging

| Layer | How |
| --- | --- |
| Challenge winners and runners-up | Chosen by each sponsor using its own criteria. **AstraZeneca:** Actionability 30%, Insight 25%, Data quality & transparency 20%, Execution (works in a live demo in front of judges) 15%, Clarity (decision-maker understands message and action in 2 minutes) 10%. Source: [/challenges/344](https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/challenges/344) |
| Overall Junction winner (€10,000; pool €100,000) | Peer review picks the **Top 5**. Criteria: **Innovation** (new vs. others), **Impact** (size of effect and share of affected people), **Implementation** (realistic, viable, feasible). 7 judges then vote after the pitches. |

## Live pitch (Top 5 only)

- **3 minutes** per team, then **3 minutes of judge questions**. Microphones are cut at the time limit.
- One hand-held mic per team; one presenter recommended, more allowed.
- Demo from your **own laptop** on stage; connection is checked beforehand.
- No separate practice time after the review period: rehearse now. Teams are told or fetched after peer review.
- Pitch deck PDF is uploaded in the submission.

## Side (tech) challenges: eligibility and rules

- You may enter **any number** of tech challenges alongside your one main challenge. Mention how you used the tech. (Announcement, 13 Nov 21:28; guide.)
- Gemini and Gemma: participants must be **18+** for Google-provided accounts. Gemini credits up to 400 teams.
- Nemotron requires the **Nemotron 3.5 Super VL** model; Context.dev and Oxylabs require a **live runtime call** in the core flow; Modal and Verda need real use on their platform.
- Prize rules: MIT license for all submissions; partner APIs remain under partner terms.
- Hardware tech challenges (Arduino, Atech) need physical demos. Not for us.

## Team rules

- Team size **1–4**. Teams are formed in the platform's Team tab with a team code. One member creates the project submission.
- Everyone registers individually and keeps the badge visible. Valid ID needed.
- Sleeping at the venue is not allowed; the venue is open 24/7 until Sunday 18:00. Sauna, lounges, food on floors 2–3.
- Code of conduct and safe-space guardians exist; emergency number 112. Contacts are on the guide page.
- Questions: Discord (`#announcements`, challenge channels) and hello@hackjunction.com (event organiser address from the guide).
