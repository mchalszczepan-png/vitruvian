# Junction 2026 – AstraZeneca track brief

Saved verbatim-in-substance from the challenge page (2026-11-13).

## The challenge

Help ensure equitable and effective cancer care for everyone in Finland.

Identify where the cancer care pathway can be strengthened: earlier detection, smoother referrals between health centres and hospitals, or faster treatment initiation.

Build a nationwide, AI-powered system that supports equal and effective cancer care across Finland. Time to diagnosis should be optimal and patients should start treatment within target timeframes, wherever they live.

The solution should:

1. **Identify the gap.** Detect differences in access to cancer care or treatment outcomes.
2. **Locate the gap in the care pathway.** Show where the difference emerges: detection, referral from primary to specialist care, diagnosis, or treatment initiation.
3. **Recommend what to do (most important).** Concrete, evidence-based improvement proposals that could be implemented at national or wellbeing-services-county level. Diagnosing is not enough.

## Possible formats (open)

- AI analyst that explains indicator trends and proposes actions
- Monitoring dashboard with recommendations
- Early-warning system for emerging delays
- Simulation of an intervention's potential impact

## Minimum requirements

- **Reliable evidence:** public data and/or AstraZeneca mentor insights. Show the source for every claim.
- **Goal 3:** cover at least one indicator connected to Goal 3 of the national cancer strategy (Kansallinen syöpästrategia 2026–2035).
- **Actionable recommendations**, not just analysis.
- **Working demo.** Slides alone are not enough.

## Known bottlenecks (starting points)

1. **Late detection:** cancer not identified early enough.
2. **Primary → specialist care transition:** referrals delayed or not progressing smoothly.
3. **Diagnosis → treatment:** patients not starting treatment within recommended target timeframes.

## Data note

Data on cancer care in Finland is limited; addressing the data gap is part of the challenge. Combine public sources with expert insights. Public, aggregate data only; no patient-level data.

A strong solution makes a complex situation clear and actionable for a non-technical decision-maker in **under two minutes**.

## Beyond Junction

AstraZeneca wants to explore promising solutions further, ideally a follow-up project with a Finnish wellbeing services county (hyvinvointialue).
